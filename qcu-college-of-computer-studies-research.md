# QCU and College of Computer Studies: brand research

Research date: 2026-10-28. Sources were searched and scraped using the local Firecrawl CLI; the links below identify the underlying sources. Institutional statements are summarized to keep this reference concise. Recheck leadership, programs and official guidance before publication.

## Identity and scope

- **Quezon City University (QCU)** is a local university serving Quezon City. Its College of Computer Studies is referred to by the university as **CCS**. [QCU college page](https://qcu.edu.ph/academics/colleges-and-courses-offered/college-of-information-technology/)
- In this project, **ACCS** means **Access for College of Computer Studies**, a project identity rather than the verified official name of the college. Its proposed tagline is “Your Access to the College of Computer Studies.” [Local brand reference](../accs-landing/BRAND_REFERENCE.md)
- QCU lists San Bartolome as its main campus, alongside San Francisco and Batasan campuses. [Quezon City Government](https://quezoncity.gov.ph/quezon-city-university-qcu/)

## Vision, mission and values

- **University vision:** international recognition as a local university committed to innovation, sustainability and positive social impact.
- **University mission:** comprehensive education that improves students’ lives and prepares them for nation-building and global citizenship.
- **Shared values — J.O.Y.:** jointness of undertakings, organizational adaptability, and a yoke of efficiency and effectiveness. These are university-wide values, not a separately verified college values list. [QCU vision and mission](https://qcu.edu.ph/about/vision-mission-strategic-goals/)
- **CCS vision:** develop adaptable, employable and industry-ready graduates with strong computing foundations, innovation and research capabilities.
- **CCS mission:** deliver rigorous, practical technology education that strengthens research, professional conduct, ethics, innovation and lifelong learning. [QCU CCS](https://qcu.edu.ph/academics/colleges-and-courses-offered/college-of-information-technology/)
- **College mantra:** “Innovate. Integrate. Empower. Impact.” QCU identifies this as the CCS mantra in a 2026 college article; it should not be presented as an official university-wide motto. [QCU CCS capstone and OJT article](https://qcu.edu.ph/qcu-ccs-prepares-students-for-innovation-and-industry/)

## Academic programs and competencies

The college lists three undergraduate degrees: [BS Information Technology, BS Computer Science and BS Information Systems](https://qcu.edu.ph/academics/colleges-and-courses-offered/college-of-information-technology/).

- **BSIT:** selecting, integrating, operating and managing hardware, software and IT infrastructure to meet organizational and user needs.
- **BSCS:** computing theory, algorithms, complex software and software engineering.
- **BSIS:** information systems, organizational processes and data-informed solutions bridging technical and management needs.

The college’s performance indicators and program outcomes emphasize capstone or thesis work, requirements specifications, object-oriented solutions, development tools, teamwork, public communication, industry immersion, independent learning, professional ethics and social responsibility. QCU also highlights research, innovation, critical thinking and practical solutions for communities in its [CCS capstone and OJT article](https://qcu.edu.ph/qcu-ccs-prepares-students-for-innovation-and-industry/).

## Colors and visual direction

The following are **ACCS project brand-reference swatches**, not independently verified official QCU or CCS department colors:

| Project swatch | Hex | Suggested use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Blue | `#032E87` | Primary identity and key accents |
| Dark navy | `#172033` | Text, contrast and depth |
| White | `#FFFFFF` | Clean backgrounds and negative space |

The [local ACCS brand reference](../accs-landing/BRAND_REFERENCE.md) records those colors from the project’s Figma branding frame. Cyan, pale blue and cobalt are useful accents in the current asset briefs, but that reference does not establish their exact hex values. It also records the linked-C logo as a **proposal**, not an approved institutional logo. No authoritative CCS department color specification was verified in this research.

For imagery, derive concepts from connectivity, algorithms, systems, human collaboration, research and practical impact. Use accessible contrast and restrained technical motifs; keep references to sustainability and public service grounded in the university’s stated priorities. These are design interpretations, not official institutional standards.
